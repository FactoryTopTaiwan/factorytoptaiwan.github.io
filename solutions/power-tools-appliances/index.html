    Industry

    Power Tool & Appliance Motors

    High-volume universal, brushed and brushless motors where cycle time decides the margin. The armature and stator route we have built since the 1990s is aimed squarely at production at rate.

    Power tools and home appliances are a volume game: the margin is set by cycle time and first-pass yield across millions of near-identical motors. The equipment has to run the rate without an operator standing at it.

    Our armature and stator route — winding, slot insulation and wedge insertion, commutator work, fusing, turning, balancing, varnish and test — was built for exactly this. The TWM-702 armature winder became the machine most Taiwanese motor plants of its class run.

    What we bring

    Built for rate

    The armature and stator machines are specified around cycle time and first-pass yield, not a one-off demonstration.

    The whole route

    Winding through to turning, balancing, varnish and test — linked into a line rather than sourced piece by piece.

    Proven at scale

    Machines like the TWM-702 armature winder run across a class of motor plants, not a single customer.
