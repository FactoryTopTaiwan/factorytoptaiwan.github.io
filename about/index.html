    History

    Thirty-plus years, in the order it happened

    1. 1992–1993
      The company opens in Taichung with a single purpose: take the manual work out of motor assembly. A winding machine is the first thing it builds.
    2. 2001
      Individual machines give way to a linked line. It is the first of the plant projects that listed manufacturers would later bring us in on.
    3. 2005–2006
      The TWM-702 armature winder becomes the machine most Taiwanese motor plants of its class are running.
    4. 2006
      Sealing and testing equipment ships to a semiconductor customer. A second industry, held to a tighter tolerance than motors ask for.
    5. 2023–2024
      A production line built specifically for drone motors. The second one goes to a customer in the United States.
    6. 2023–2024
      Hairpin stator work starts, prototyping with customers. Hairpin is what EV traction motors are wound with, so this is where the next decade is.
    7. 2025–2026
      FaTop Automation opens as the route for buyers outside Taiwan to reach us directly.

    Do you run a Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) before shipment?

    Yes. We agree measurable criteria — cycle time, first-pass yield, defect handling — and demonstrate them on your parts on our floor. You can attend in person or witness by live video, and we provide the FAT report.
