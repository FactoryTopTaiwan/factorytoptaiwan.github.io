    Privacy policy

    This website collects nothing. There is no form, no account, no analytics and no cookie. Enquiries reach us as ordinary email, and this page says exactly what happens to them.

    Who we are

    Teamwork Automation Machinery Co., Ltd., No. 46-2, Lane 53, Shengli 8th Street, Tanzi District, Taichung, Taiwan. Questions about anything on this page go to iverson.hsu@fatop-global.com.

    What this website collects

    Nothing. The site has no contact form, no account, no login and no basket. It runs no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts, and it sets no cookies.

    There is nothing here that gathers your details, because there is nowhere here to submit them.

    When you email or telephone us

    Enquiries reach us directly, not through this website. What you send - your name, your company, your country, your contact details, and whatever you tell us about the part you need to produce - arrives in our company mailbox and is used to answer you and to prepare a quotation.

    We do not build a customer database from website visitors. We do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for. We do not sell, rent or trade contact details to anyone.

    How long we keep it

    Enquiry correspondence is kept while the enquiry or the commercial relationship is live, and after that for as long as our accounting and warranty obligations require.

    Your appearance preference

    If you switch the site between light and dark, that single choice is stored by your own browser under the name theme. It is local storage, not a cookie: it never leaves your device and it is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

    Hosting

    The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives the request your browser makes - which includes your IP address - and may log it for security and abuse prevention. That processing is GitHub's, under GitHub's terms. We have no access to those logs and we receive nothing from them.

    Your rights

    You can ask what correspondence we hold from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email the address above and we will answer.

    Where a deletion request conflicts with an accounting or contractual obligation we are subject to, we will tell you which records we have to keep and why, rather than quietly keeping them.

    If this ever changes

    If we add a contact form, analytics, or anywhere for submitted data to be stored, this page is updated before that goes live - not afterwards.
