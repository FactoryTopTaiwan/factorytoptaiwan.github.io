    Turnkey production lines

    One supplier, from bare wire to a tested motor.

    Most equipment vendors cover a slice of the process and leave you to integrate the rest. We build every stage — winding, insulation, insertion, fusing, turning, balancing, varnish and test — and we plan the plant itself, not only the machines in it. That is what fifty listed manufacturers worldwide brought us in to do.

    Scope

    What is included

    • Plant planned from zero: line layout, workstation count, conveying method and footprint
    • Catalogue modules wherever they fit, purpose-built equipment where they do not
    • Barcode and laser marking for unit-level traceability across the line
    • Production data collected centrally, with a floor board showing line state live
    • Remote monitoring on any PLC-equipped machine — faults diagnosed over the internet

    On the line

    What a Teamwork line does that a set of machines does not

    • Barcode and laser part marking, so every unit is traceable through the line
    • Manufacturing data collection into a central system
    • A production information board on the floor, showing line state in real time
    • Remote equipment monitoring and maintenance over the internet
