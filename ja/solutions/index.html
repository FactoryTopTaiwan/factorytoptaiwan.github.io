Solutions

The motors our machines are built around

Automotive, two-wheel and e-mobility, power tools, drone and micro BLDC, appliance, and semiconductor packaging. The machine is specified against the part, so the industry decides the tooling.

Automotive

Motors that go into a vehicle before it ever moves under its own power.

E-mobility & two-wheelers

Scooter, motorbike and electric-bike drivetrains, plus the hairpin stator work behind EV traction motors.

Power tools

High-volume universal and brushless motors where cycle time decides the margin.

Drone & micro BLDC

Out-slot and in-slot brushless stators at small diameters, where slot fill and repeatability decide flight time. First dedicated line delivered 2023, exported to the USA.

Appliance & fitness

Consumer and building equipment motors produced at consumer-market cost.

Semiconductor

Sealing and testing process equipment since 2006 — the same motion, vision and control platform held to a tighter precision class.
