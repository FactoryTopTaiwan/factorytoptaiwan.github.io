    The people who build the line behind your motor

    How we de-risk a high-ticket line

    What a global buyer actually needs to trust a supplier

    FAT before it leaves

    A documented Factory Acceptance Test on your parts, witnessed on-site or by video, against agreed cycle time and yield criteria before shipment.

    SAT after it lands

    Site Acceptance Test and commissioning at your plant, with sign-off on the same criteria under your power, air and material.

    Overseas engineer dispatch

    We send our own commissioning engineers worldwide for installation, run-off and operator training, and we handle their visa and travel logistics. Typical mobilisation is two to four weeks from site readiness.

    Remote diagnostics

    Every PLC-equipped machine supports secure remote access, so most software and parameter faults are diagnosed over the internet — often resolved without waiting for a site visit.

    Spare parts & lead time

    We supply a recommended spare-parts kit sized to your line and hold common wear items in stock, so routine replacements ship quickly rather than being built to order.

    Warranty & documentation

    One-year warranty from formal acceptance, subject to the final contract. It covers material and workmanship under normal use; consumables, unauthorised modification or parameter changes and missed scheduled maintenance are excluded, and any replacement parts fitted afterward carry their own one-year warranty. Delivery includes full operation manuals with I/O lists and electrical and pneumatic schematics, plus seaworthy export packing on wooden crates with vacuum wrapping.

    International buyer FAQ

    The questions purchasing and engineering ask us first

    Straight answers for buyers evaluating a turnkey partner from overseas. Where a number depends on the project, we say so rather than quote a figure we cannot stand behind.

    Do you run a Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) before shipment?

    Yes. We agree measurable criteria — cycle time, first-pass yield, defect handling — and demonstrate them on your parts on our floor. You can attend in person or witness by live video, and we provide the FAT report.

    What about Site Acceptance Test (SAT) and installation?

    We commission the line at your plant and re-run the acceptance criteria under your utilities and materials. You provide rigging, utilities and consumables; on-site commissioning typically runs one to two weeks depending on line scope.

    Can you send engineers overseas for installation and training?

    Yes. We dispatch our own engineers worldwide for installation, run-off and operator training, and we handle visa and travel logistics. Typical mobilisation is two to four weeks from site readiness.

    What are typical lead times?

    Lead time depends on specification and current backlog: roughly eight to sixteen weeks for a single standard machine, and four to eight months for a modular or turnkey line.

    How is after-sales support handled across time zones?

    Support is by email and phone during Taiwan business hours (GMT+8), with secure remote access to PLC-equipped machines for fast diagnosis between visits.

    What is the warranty?

    One year from formal acceptance, covering defects in material and workmanship under normal use. Consumables and wear parts, unauthorised modification or parameter changes, and missed scheduled maintenance are excluded. Replacement parts fitted after the warranty carry their own one-year warranty. Exact terms follow the final order contract.

    What payment terms and currency do you work in?

    We typically work in USD, on T/T with staged milestones tied to order, shipment and acceptance; letters of credit can be arranged for larger orders. Final terms are set in the order contract.

    Can the line integrate with our MES / data systems?

    Our lines carry barcode and laser marking for unit-level traceability, and production data is collected centrally with a live floor board showing line state. Data export and MES / OPC-UA integration are configured to your plant's systems.

    Do you protect our process and IP?

    Yes. We work under NDA and do not disclose customer processes, part geometries or line photographs. That is also why our public references are anonymized.
